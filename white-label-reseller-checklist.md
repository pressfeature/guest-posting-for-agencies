# White-Label Reseller Checklist: Vetting a Placement Provider

Run this checklist before you resell anyone's guest post or sponsored article placements to your clients. A provider that fails any item in Section A is not white-label ready. Section B items decide your margin and your sanity.

## Section A: Deal-breakers

- [ ] The provider names the exact outlet before you pay, not a category like "a major US news site."
- [ ] Article, price, timeline and link policy are confirmed in writing before payment.
- [ ] A full refund if the article does not go live is promised in writing, with the exact wording saved.
- [ ] Turnaround is stated per outlet in writing, not "soon" or "ASAP."
- [ ] The provider confirms how the outlet handles sponsored labelling, in writing, before publication.
- [ ] The provider communicates only with your agency, never directly with your client.
- [ ] You receive the live link on the outlet's own domain as the deliverable, not screenshots.

## Section B: Margin and sanity

- [ ] You know the provider's price before you quote the client, so your markup is real math, not a guess.
- [ ] You have tested the process with one small order before selling it to a big client.
- [ ] The provider offers an outlet picker or shortlist you can show the client without revealing the supply chain.
- [ ] Revisions policy is clear: who writes the article, how many revision rounds, who approves the final copy.
- [ ] Link policy is specific: which pages can be linked, how many links, whether links pass search value.
- [ ] You have a written turnaround date you can quote the client, plus your own buffer on top.
- [ ] The provider has one point of contact and one team handling the placement end to end.
- [ ] You know what happens if the client rejects the article copy: rewrite, revision, or refund.

## Section C: After delivery

- [ ] File the live link, the written approval, and the refund terms per client, in one folder.
- [ ] Report the placement to the client with the template in `templates/client-delivery-template.md`.
- [ ] Ask the client for permission to show the placement in your portfolio and case studies.
- [ ] Add "featured on news sites" to your service page as a standard line item once the first placement lands.
