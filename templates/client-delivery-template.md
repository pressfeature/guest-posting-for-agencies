# Client Delivery Template: Reporting a Placement

Copy this template, fill in the bracketed fields, and send it to your client when their placement goes live. It is written for your agency's letterhead: the client sees your work, not the supply chain.

---

Subject: Your feature is live on [Outlet Name]

Hi [Client Name],

Your article is live. Here are the details:

- **Outlet:** [Outlet Name]
- **Article title:** [Article Title]
- **Live link:** [Full URL on the outlet's domain]
- **Publication date:** [Date]

The copy is exactly what you approved. Nothing was changed after your sign-off.

**What to do with it now:**

1. Add the live link to your website's press page.
2. Add the outlet's logo to your homepage "As Featured On" strip. You earned it, show it.
3. Link the article from your next investor update, pitch deck, or sales proposal.
4. Share it on your social channels and tag the outlet.

**A note on expectations:** this feature builds trust and opens doors. It puts a recognized news name next to your brand, which shortens sales conversations and strengthens every other marketing you run. It is not a traffic faucet, and we never promised one.

If you want a second placement on another outlet, reply to this email and we will line up the options.

Best,
[Your Name]
[Your Agency]

---

*Template notes for your team (delete before sending): confirm the sponsored labelling with the client if they ask, keep the written approval and the live link filed per client, and ask the client for permission to feature this placement in your portfolio.*
